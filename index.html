VL Reports

Advertising performance reporting by Vadim Litvinenko

VL Reports is a private, read-only reporting tool used by Vadim Litvinenko, a performance marketing specialist, to analyze advertising accounts that he owns or manages for his clients.

The tool reads campaign statistics (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and similar metrics) from Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts the signed-in user already has access to, and turns them into reports and recommendations. It never creates, edits or pauses campaigns and does not access any accounts the user has not been granted access to.

It is not a public service: only the owner signs in.
